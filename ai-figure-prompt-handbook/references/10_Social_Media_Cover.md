# Social Media Cover

Use for X/Twitter, LinkedIn, WeChat, blog, or project-page cover images derived from research work.

## Narrative Shape

Use visual immediacy:

1. Topic identity.
2. One striking research object or result.
3. Minimal method cue.
4. Optional title/venue marker.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a social media cover for research on [TOPIC]. The cover should make [KEY IDEA] immediately recognizable while remaining academically credible.

Use a widescreen or square composition optimized for feed preview. Use one dominant visual object, one short title, and at most two supporting labels. Use clean vector/editorial style, strong contrast, and restrained colors. Leave safe margins for cropping.

Avoid dense architecture diagrams, tiny text, inflated claims, generic AI sparkle effects, and stock-photo aesthetics.
```

## Design Checks

- Does it read instantly on a phone?
- Is the scientific subject visible without caption support?
- Is the tone credible rather than promotional?
