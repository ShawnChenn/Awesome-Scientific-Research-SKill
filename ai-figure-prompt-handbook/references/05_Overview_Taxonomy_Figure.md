# Overview Taxonomy Figure

Use for survey maps, taxonomy diagrams, conceptual landscapes, method families, or categorization figures.

## Narrative Shape

Use hierarchy:

1. Top-level field or problem.
2. Major categories.
3. Representative subtypes or examples.
4. Position of the user's work, if applicable.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create an overview taxonomy figure for [FIELD/TOPIC]. The figure should organize the space into [N] major categories: [CATEGORIES].

Use a hierarchical layout such as a tree, matrix, radial map, or layered cards. Keep categories visually balanced and use semantic grouping through color bands, dividers, and icon families. If [OUR METHOD] is included, mark it with a subtle highlight and explain its position in the taxonomy.

Use concise labels and avoid overcrowding. The figure should help readers understand the structure of the research area, not list every paper.
```

## Design Checks

- Are categories mutually understandable and visually separated?
- Is the hierarchy clearer than a bullet list?
- Is the highlighted work contextualized without looking promotional?
